About me

I’m Jingyao Lu, an MSc student in Computer Science & Engineering at Eindhoven University of Technology and a machine learning intern at Loadmaster.ai. My research focuses on reinforcement learning for sequential decision-making. I’m particularly interested in how hierarchical and multi-agent policies can solve problems such as container placement under operational constraints. My previous work explored how reinforcement learning can help language models decide what to ask next in a dialogue.
